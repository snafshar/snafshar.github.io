Your privacy

Privacy & visit statistics

This photography journal is managed by Sina Afshar. You can contact the owner through the Contact page.

Optional visitor statistics

Statistics are currently enabled, with your permission. The owner uses these statistics to understand which photographs and stories people visit and how they find the journal. You can browse all public pages while declining. No visit statistics are collected before you allow them.

After you allow statistics, the journal records the time, page or published post you open, your IP address, approximate country / region / city when the hosting service provides them, and a device category, browser and operating system inferred from the browser's request. The arrival source uses the referring website's host name or the utm_source and utm_campaign labels in a link. Full referring URLs, search terms, contact-form contents and precise GPS coordinates are excluded.

Cookies and retention

First-party identifiers identify a consenting browser (up to 180 days) and a browsing session (30 minutes of inactivity). They use local browser storage on GitHub Pages and HttpOnly cookies on the original host. Your allow / decline preference is stored on this browser for up to 180 days. Detailed visit data is retained for 30 days. Older data is excluded from reports and pruned when the statistics service is used. A change to the retention or IP storage setting asks for fresh consent.

Your choice

Use to allow or disable collection. Disabling also deletes the visit history associated with this browser's current statistics identifier. If you have cleared that identifier, contact the owner with the dates and IP address you want them to check. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control requests disable new collection. Signed-in owner visits and recognised bots are excluded.

Access and limits

Visit history is available only in the owner's protected dashboard and is stored with this website's hosting services. This journal loads no third-party analytics service. Browser blocking, missing referrers, shared devices, VPNs and cookie deletion affect the reports. A visitor count estimates browsers, not identified people. Location is approximate.

Other information you send

If you use the contact form, your name, email and message are saved privately for the owner to read and reply. These messages are separate from visit statistics. External contact links open their respective providers, which apply their own privacy policies. The owner can help with questions, access or deletion requests. You may also raise a concern with your local data-protection authority.
