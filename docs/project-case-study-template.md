# Project Case Study Template

## Problem
Who experiences the problem, and why does it matter?

## Constraints
List technical, time, data, and accessibility constraints.

## Approach
Explain the architecture and key trade-offs.

## Implementation
Describe the most important components and interfaces.

## Validation
List tests, manual checks, and known limitations.

## Outcome
Report measured results where available; do not invent metrics.

## Next steps
Name the next improvement and why it matters.
